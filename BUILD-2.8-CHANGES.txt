Build 2.8 — Family Units
- Built from stable 2.6.2.1 base.
- Tree overview anchors on the signed-in person's parental family unit instead of treating every no-parent spouse as an ancestor.
- Priscilla/Lloyd-style spouses no longer become roots simply because their parents are unknown.
- Maternal and paternal grandparent units are rendered separately above their actual child side.
- Couple blocks are darker and clickable.
- Clicking a couple opens a focused family branch.
- Focused branch shows only children explicitly connected to either partner and states which parent connection is known.
- Spouse never implies parent.
- Member-card relationship titles are removed from the directory while data remains stored.
- Contact number support retained.
