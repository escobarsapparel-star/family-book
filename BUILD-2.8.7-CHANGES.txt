Build 2.8.7
TREE GEOMETRY
- Rebuilt connector positioning around fixed branch-column centers.
- Main sibling rail now starts/ends at the actual center of the first/last child family unit.
- Every parent-to-child vertical drop uses that same center.
- Descendant rails use the same rule.
- Single-child branches no longer draw a meaningless horizontal stub.

CLICK BEHAVIOUR
- Grey/tan couple block or heart: opens that couple's focused family branch.
- In a focused branch, grey/tan couple background: returns to the full Family Tree.
- White person card/name inside a couple: opens that person's View Member/contact card.
- Individual person card/name on the Tree: opens View Member, not Edit.
- Members directory avatar/name: opens View Member.
- Members three-dot menu remains the place for Edit Member and Remove Member.

No relationship data was changed.
