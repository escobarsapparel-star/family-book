FAMILY BOOK — BUILD 5.0.1
Remove Family Access
Date: 2026-09-14

NEW ADMIN CONTROL
- Family Admin can now remove a connected family account from:
  Settings -> Invite & family access -> Connected accounts.
- Each other account has a "Remove access" button.
- A Family Admin cannot remove their own account from this screen.

IMPORTANT SEPARATION
Removing family access DOES NOT delete the person.
It removes only the account/family-access layer:
- removes the connected account from the family account registry
- clears accountId from the linked Member profile
- keeps the Member profile
- keeps Family Tree relationships
- keeps Memories/tags
- keeps Wall posts, reactions and comments
- keeps calendar/family history data

WHY
A person and an account are separate concepts in Family Book.
If an account was created or linked by mistake, the safe first action is to
disconnect it without destroying family data.

IF THE MEMBER PROFILE WAS ALSO CREATED BY MISTAKE
1. Remove access from Invite & family access.
2. Go to Family -> Members.
3. The profile is now unclaimed.
4. Family Admin can use Remove member to delete that profile and its tree links.

SESSION REVOCATION
- A removed joined account can no longer sign back in through the local account registry.
- If that account is currently open in another normal browser tab, the localStorage
  account change is detected and that tab reloads to the signed-out state.

FUTURE BACKEND
Production terminology should remain "Remove family access" / "Remove from family",
not "Delete account", because the person's global authentication account may belong
to other families/services and should not be destroyed by a family admin.
