# FamilyBook Marketing Brand Guide

## Product name
FamilyBook

Display wording: **Family Book** in marketing copy when the two-word brand treatment is preferred.
Pronunciation: normal English “Family Book”.

## Approved tagline
**Our family. Our memories. Our story.**

Supporting approved line used in the product:
**Family is everything.**

## Brand colours
- Primary green: #315C43
- Dark green: #244A35
- Warm brown accent: #A66B38
- Cream background: #F8F3E9
- Paper / warm white: #FFFDFA
- Main ink: #20251F
- Muted text: #6F756E
- Divider / border: #E5DDD1

## Typography
Primary interface font: **Inter**
Use Inter for headings, body copy, navigation, buttons and captions.
Supported weights in the current product: 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900.

## Demo identity
Use **fictional/anonymised demo names only** in public promotional material.
Current public demo family: **Williams Family**.
Do not use real FamilyBook member names or private family data.

## Product positioning
FamilyBook is a private family space for:
- family profiles
- family relationships and family tree
- family wall / family updates
- photo and video memories
- birthdays and family calendar events
- Family Fun camera and family video experiences

## Marketing accuracy
Use screenshots and footage from the public Guest Demo.
Do not imply that public demo content is a real family.
Do not expose or request production login credentials.
